01 · Collision

          
What does one collision look like?

          
Every batch of data starts here: two beams cross at IP1, energy turns into fragments, and the detector's layers record the tracks flying outward. Press "Run collision" to play a full event in the 3D scene on the left.

          

            
From collision to data

            

              A single collision never gives a direct answer. Physicists slice thousands of collisions into
              time-ordered batches called "chunks." Each chunk is summarized into a lightweight report
              (event count, mass distribution, etc.) and handed to the DQM Agent, which decides whether
              that batch of data can be trusted. The next step is to see what those chunks look like.

03 · Agent picks its tools

          
It doesn't run every check, every time

          
The Agent only ever sees a lightweight summary of a chunk -- never the raw measurements. From that summary alone it decides which of four checks are worth actually running, corrects for having run several at once, and compares against recent history for a developing trend.
